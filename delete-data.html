Delete Your Account and Data

Privacy Policy Terms of Service & EULA
InkedApp (com.kutluca.inkedapp) · Last updated: 7 August 2026

You can delete your InkedApp account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it, and you do not need the app installed to make a request.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open InkedApp and go to your desk.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Data and Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email kutlucalabs@icloud.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my InkedApp account”. Include your Passport ID (for example UN-AR-584954-33) if you know it. We will confirm within 7 days and then follow the same timeline described below.

What happens, and when

WhenWhat happens
Immediately Your account is deactivated. Your profile stops appearing on the compass, nobody can find you or write to you, and you are signed out.
Within 30 days You can change your mind. Sign in again with your email and password and your account is restored exactly as you left it, with all of your letters.
After 30 days Everything listed below is permanently deleted by an automated process. This cannot be undone.
If you never added an email address to your account, it cannot be restored once you confirm deletion — signing back in requires an email and password. Please be sure before you confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: display name, Passport ID, biography, country, region and interests
  • Your email address and date of birth
  • Every letter you have sent and received, including drafts and letters in transit
  • Your notice-board post
  • Your blocked-users list
  • Your Ink Drop balance and your unlocked paper, ink and envelope collection
  • Your sign-in credentials (the authentication record itself)
A note about your correspondents. Deleting your account removes your letters from your pen pals’ archives as well, because a letter cannot exist with no one on one end of it. This is different from deleting a single letter inside the app, which only removes it from your own archive.

What is kept, and why

DataReason
Reports of abuse filed by you or about you Retained after review for as long as necessary to enforce our rules and to detect repeat offenders. If these were erased on request, a user who broke the rules could clear their history simply by deleting their account.
Purchase and transaction records Held by Google Play and the Apple App Store for tax and accounting purposes. These are outside our control — please contact the store directly.
Encrypted backups Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period after deletion, and are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

We do not sell your data, and we do not keep a usable copy of a deleted account. For the full picture, see our Privacy Policy.
