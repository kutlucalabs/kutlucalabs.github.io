This Privacy Policy explains how Kutluca Labs ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications (collectively, "the Applications").

Our Applications fall into two groups, and they are treated very differently by this policy:

  • Games — HuePanic, GLYPHIX, MagneBlock and similar titles. No account, no login, no user profile.
  • InkedApp — a pen-pal application. Requires an account and involves communication between users.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Games (HuePanic, GLYPHIX, MagneBlock)

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information in our games. There is no account, no login, and no profile. These games do not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not do so. The only data processed is that described in Section 4 (advertising) and Section 5 (diagnostics).

To provide pen-pal matching and letter delivery, we collect the following:

2.3 What we do not collect

  • No photographs or images. InkedApp has no photo-sharing feature. We do not access your camera or photo library.
  • No precise location. Country is a value you select yourself. We do not use GPS or device location services.
  • No contacts, microphone, or calendar access.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and deliver letters and other core features you have asked for.
  • Consent — for personalised advertising and, on iOS, for tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 4).
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service safe and functioning: automated content screening, handling reports and blocks, preventing abuse, and diagnosing crashes.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with applicable law.

4. Advertising, Consent, and Tracking

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded video ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4.1 Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Before ads are loaded, we present a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or refuse consent for the purposes offered. You can reopen this form at any time from the app's settings and change your choice.

4.2 App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

This applies to InkedApp only. Our games do not request tracking permission and do not track you.

On iOS, InkedApp asks for your permission before tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, we do not track you and you will see non-personalised advertising instead; the app remains fully usable. You can change this decision at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Diagnostics and Analytics

We collect non-personal diagnostic data, such as crash reports and basic error logs, to identify and fix defects. This data is processed through Google Firebase and is not used to identify you or to build a profile about you.

6. Automated Content Screening

To protect users — particularly younger ones — text written in InkedApp (letters and notice-board posts) is automatically screened on your device before it is sent. The screening looks for profanity, sexual content, and attempts to exchange personal contact details such as phone numbers or social-media handles.

If a message is blocked, you are told and may edit and resend it. This screening is automated and happens locally; the content of a blocked message is not transmitted to us. It does not produce any legal or similarly significant effect for you, and it does not replace human review of reports.

7. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users — your nickname, country, interests, and the letters or posts you choose to send.
  • With service providers — Google Firebase (authentication, database) and Google AdMob (advertising), acting on our behalf.
  • With app stores — Apple and Google process your in-app purchases. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Where legally required — to comply with a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or of Kutluca Labs.

8. International Data Transfers

Our services run on Google Cloud infrastructure and your data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved safeguard, as implemented by Google.

10. Automated Accounts

To keep InkedApp usable while its community is still growing, some profiles and notice-board posts are generated automatically by us rather than written by real people. These accounts exist only to populate the app; they are not real individuals, and any correspondence with them is machine-generated. No personal data of yours is shared with a third party through them.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data — your nickname, country, and interests can be edited in the app at any time;
  • delete your account and associated data, using the in-app account deletion feature or by contacting us;
  • withdraw consent to personalised advertising or tracking, at any time and without penalty;
  • object to or request restriction of processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • request portability of the data you provided to us;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to kutlucalabs@icloud.com. We respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before acting on a request.

Note that your date of birth cannot be changed after registration. It is used to enforce our minimum age requirement, and allowing it to be edited would defeat that purpose. If it was entered incorrectly, contact us.

12. Children and Minimum Age

InkedApp is not for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an InkedApp account.

We ask for your date of birth during registration and refuse registration to anyone under 16. Our games are not directed to children and do not collect personal information from anyone.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us at kutlucalabs@icloud.com and we will delete the account and its data promptly.

13. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Firebase with access restricted by server-side security rules, so that one user cannot read another user's private data. Authentication credentials are handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
